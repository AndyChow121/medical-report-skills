# Chinese Medical Literature Report PPT Standard

## Goal

Create a professional, fresh, source-faithful Chinese medical literature report that an audience can follow without reading the article first. Let article complexity and the user's limit determine slide count. The final slide should be a conventional closing slide.

## Required Opening

Include the applicable items:

- journal name and latest verified journal metric with metric year and source;
- Chinese report title and exact English article title;
- authors, corresponding author, and main institutions;
- year, volume, issue, pages, DOI, PMID/PMCID or registry identifier;
- article type and study design;
- contents page;
- structured abstract or article overview.

## Core Narrative

1. Article profile and structured abstract.
2. Research background and current evidence.
3. Evidence gap and objective or research question.
4. Study architecture and timeline.
5. Population or biological model, source, inclusion, and exclusion.
6. Intervention, exposure, index test, specimens, methods, platforms, and outcome definitions.
7. Statistical or computational method in plain language.
8. Main results with exact values and original evidence objects.
9. Secondary, subgroup, sensitivity, harm, or null results when important.
10. Authors' interpretation and comparison with prior evidence.
11. Strengths, limitations, applicability, and critical appraisal.
12. Professional implications labeled as inference.
13. Take-home messages and closing slide.

Adapt the sequence to the design. For example, meta-analyses need search and heterogeneity; diagnostic studies need index/reference tests and accuracy measures; basic studies need experimental model and validation; guidelines need evidence-to-recommendation methods.

## Complex Method Explanation

Explain unfamiliar methods as:

```text
Input data -> what the method does -> grouping or parameter rule -> validation -> output meaning
```

Common examples include randomization, propensity scores, multivariable models, ROC analysis, calibration, mediation, interaction, survival analysis, meta-analysis, unsupervised clustering, single-cell sequencing, multi-omics, and machine learning.

## Result Slide Contract

Each result slide should contain:

- a conclusion-style title;
- one dominant original figure, table, or exact editable evidence object;
- concise Chinese interpretation;
- concrete numbers, denominators, time points, and comparison groups;
- a source note naming the original figure, table, appendix, or supplement.

Use original figures from the article or supplements. Cropping, enlargement, and panel separation are allowed only when labels and context remain accurate. Do not alter values, axes, group labels, significance markers, or inconvenient data.

Editable tables and simple diagrams may reorganize exact source content. Label them `根据原文整理` and preserve the source citation.

## Evidence Labels

Use quiet, consistent labels:

- `原文结果`: directly reported data or conclusion;
- `中文转述`: faithful professional paraphrase;
- `作者解释`: interpretation or mechanism proposed by the authors;
- `外部证据`: evidence from cited or separately verified sources;
- `延伸解读`: presenter inference based on the paper.

## Visual System

- Use 16:9 unless the user specifies otherwise.
- Keep the style professional, clean, restrained, and appropriate to the specialty.
- Use visible chapter transitions so the audience knows the current section.
- Use more than one restrained color family; avoid a monotonous single-hue deck.
- Keep original figures visually dominant and readable.
- Do not repeat the same layout for three consecutive slides.
- Avoid decorative gradients, floating-card overload, unrelated stock imagery, and ornamental medical icons.
- Do not reuse another paper's exact layout; vary composition, palette, and rhythm while preserving quality.
- Keep titles, page numbers, source notes, evidence labels, and terminology consistent.

## Versioning And QA

- Preserve the previous deck and save revisions separately.
- Render every slide and inspect a contact sheet plus high-risk slides at full size.
- Fix overlap, clipping, isolated punctuation, low contrast, unreadable figures, and inconsistent alignment.
- Verify page numbers, citations, figure provenance, and evidence labels.
- Confirm expected slide count and media integrity inside the PPTX package.
- Open the PPTX in Microsoft PowerPoint or a compatible renderer when available.
- Keep only requested final deliverables in the delivery folder.
