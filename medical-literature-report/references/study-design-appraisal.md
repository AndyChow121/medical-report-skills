# Study Design Appraisal

Identify the design before appraising it. Check the current version of relevant reporting or risk-of-bias guidance at task time.

## Randomized Trials

Extract randomization, allocation concealment, blinding, intervention fidelity, analysis population, attrition, protocol deviations, outcome hierarchy, multiplicity, harms, effect estimates, confidence intervals, and registration. Distinguish efficacy from effectiveness and superiority from noninferiority or equivalence.

## Observational Studies

Identify cohort, case-control, cross-sectional, registry, or routinely collected data design. Examine selection, exposure and outcome measurement, temporality, confounding, missingness, immortal-time or indication bias, model specification, sensitivity analyses, and generalizability. Association language is mandatory unless a justified causal design supports more.

## Diagnostic Accuracy Studies

Extract intended use, target condition, index test, reference standard, participant spectrum, sampling, blinding, indeterminate results, thresholds, two-by-two data, sensitivity, specificity, likelihood ratios, predictive values, and confidence intervals. Separate analytical performance from clinical diagnostic accuracy. Flag data-driven cutoffs and lack of external validation.

## Prediction And Prognostic Models

Separate model development from validation. Check sample size, events, candidate predictors, missing data, overfitting, internal validation, discrimination, calibration, clinical utility, and external validation. Do not treat AUC alone as proof of usefulness.

## Systematic Reviews And Meta-analyses

Verify protocol, eligibility, databases, complete search, duplicate screening, risk-of-bias assessment, effect measure, heterogeneity, model choice, small-study effects, sensitivity analyses, certainty assessment, and study overlap. Do not present pooled precision as protection from biased inputs.

## Guidelines And Consensus Statements

Identify sponsor, panel composition, conflicts, evidence search, certainty framework, recommendation process, external review, update policy, and recommendation strength. Separate evidence-based recommendations from expert consensus.

## Basic, Translational, Omics, And AI Studies

Check biological model, controls, replication, randomization or blinding, batch effects, preprocessing, multiple testing, validation set independence, leakage, code/data availability, mechanistic support, and translation gap. Distinguish technical validation, biological validation, and clinical validation.

## Qualitative And Mixed-methods Studies

Check sampling rationale, researcher reflexivity, data collection, coding process, triangulation, saturation, negative cases, participant context, and integration of qualitative and quantitative strands.

## Statistical Reading Sequence

For every design, explain:

```text
Question -> analysis population -> variables and definitions -> model/test -> adjustment |
effect estimate and uncertainty -> validation/sensitivity analysis -> practical meaning
```

Check denominators before percentages, confidence intervals before isolated P values, absolute effects before relative effects, and prespecified analyses before exploratory analyses.

## Appraisal Output

Use four separate headings:

1. Internal validity and risk of bias.
2. Reporting completeness and reproducibility.
3. External validity and applicability.
4. Clinical, laboratory, nursing, pharmacy, or public-health relevance.

Do not collapse these dimensions into a single vague quality label.
