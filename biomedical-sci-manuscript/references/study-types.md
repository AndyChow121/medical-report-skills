# Study Type Rules

Use these rules after determining whether the manuscript is bioinformatics, clinical, or basic biomedical research.

## Bioinformatics Manuscripts

### Scope

Use for omics, radiomics, computational biology, immune infiltration, pathway enrichment, prognostic or diagnostic modeling, subtype identification, drug prediction, causal inference, wet-lab validation attached to computational results, or integrated bioinformatics analyses.

### Introduction Outline Focus

- Disease or biological background.
- Disease burden, diagnostic or therapeutic limitations, and research necessity.
- Phenotype, exposure, gene set, molecular process, imaging feature, or analytical target.
- Prior findings related to the target.
- Relationship between the target and the disease.
- Methods used and their advantages.
- Study objective.

### Discussion Result Categories

Use only these categories:

- 影像组结果类型：影像组学特征；影像组学模型；模型效果验证；预后分析；影像组学特征与临床和分子特征的关系。
- 生物信息学结果类型：基因表达分析；通路；免疫反应；机制；相互作用；模型效果验证；差异分析；药物分子；潜在因果关系；亚型鉴定。
- 湿实验结果类型：蛋白质表达；转录因子；生存分析；免疫检查点治疗敏感性分析；药物半致死浓度；细胞培养实验结果分析。
- 病理组学结果类型：组织形态学变化；分子病理学分析；染色技术与组织化学分析。
- 代谢组学结果类型：生物标志物；时间动态分析；环境和营养影响。
- 蛋白组学结果类型：蛋白质鉴定。

### Discussion Emphasis

- Include concrete genes, pathways, immune cells, radiomics features, model metrics, survival results, datasets, p values, AUCs, or validation results when present.
- Discuss biological mechanism and clinical or translational relevance.
- Do not invent wet-lab validation if the input lacks it.

### Title Rules

Use disease, omics type, computational method, model, core gene/pathway, and major phenotype when available.

Useful patterns:

- `Combined analysis of single-cell and bulk transcriptome sequencing data identifies ...`
- `Identification of ... as an actionable therapeutic target for ... via ...`
- `An integrated bioinformatics and machine learning approach for ...`

## Clinical Manuscripts

### Scope

Use for patient cohorts, clinical diagnosis, treatments, drugs, adverse events, prognosis, biomarkers, pathology, disease mechanisms from clinical evidence, statistical analysis, and risk factor studies.

### Introduction Outline Focus

- Disease background.
- Harm to patients and society, including disease burden when verified.
- Current diagnosis and treatment options.
- Limitations in current clinical practice.
- Study phenotype, exposure, intervention, or clinical question.
- Prior clinical findings and relationship to the disease.
- Study methods, feasibility, and clinical value.
- Study objective.

### Discussion Result Categories

Use only these categories:

- 患者特征
- 治疗方案
- 药物效果
- 副作用
- 预后评估
- 生物标志物
- 病理特征
- 疾病机制
- 统计分析
- 风险因素

### Discussion Emphasis

- Detail patient demographics, disease course, treatment comparisons, effect sizes, statistical significance, adverse events, survival outcomes, biomarker performance, pathology findings, and risk models when present.
- Explain clinical mechanisms, patient benefit, quality-of-life relevance, clinical decision-making value, and cost or resource implications when supported.
- In discussion outlines, include literature comparison suggestions but do not invent specific literature findings before literature is available.

### Title Rules

Use the disease, patient population, intervention/exposure/biomarker, study design, and core clinical endpoint when available. Prefer concise clinical research titles that clearly express the clinical question.

## Basic Biomedical Manuscripts

### Scope

Use for molecular mechanism, signaling pathway, gene function, cell behavior, immune mechanisms, biochemistry, pharmacology, structural biology, development, pathophysiology, systems biology, microbiology, neuroscience, environmental biology, metabolism, epigenetics, stem cells, cancer biology, biomaterials, nanotechnology, genome editing, and synthetic biology studies.

### Introduction Outline Focus

- The specific biological or biomedical question.
- Importance of the question in biology or medicine.
- Current research status, gaps, or controversies.
- Study object: molecule, pathway, cell type, immune mechanism, disease model, material, or experimental system.
- Prior findings in the field.
- Relationship between the object and the study problem.
- Methods, method advantages, and study objective.

### Discussion Result Categories

Use only these categories:

- 分子机制
- 信号通路
- 基因功能
- 细胞行为
- 免疫机制
- 生物化学分析
- 药理学
- 生物信息学
- 结构生物学
- 发育生物学
- 病理生理学
- 系统生物学
- 微生物学
- 神经生物学
- 环境生物学
- 代谢
- 表观遗传学
- 干细胞
- 癌生物学
- 生物材料和纳米技术
- 基因组编辑
- 合成生物学

### Discussion Emphasis

- Detail molecular processes, regulatory nodes, pathway direction, downstream effects, gene function and expression, cell phenotype, biochemical evidence, pharmacological effects, or model-system results when present.
- Explain how findings improve understanding of basic biology, disease mechanism, drug development, or therapeutic strategy.
- Compare mechanisms with prior literature and identify agreement, conflict, or plausible reasons for differences.

### Title Rules

Generate one rigorous SCI-style basic research title. Include:

- core biological function, such as activation, inhibition, regulation, remodeling, or reprogramming;
- specific molecular mechanism, such as signaling pathway, post-translational modification, transcriptional regulation, metabolic control, or cell-cell interaction;
- final phenotypic effect or disease relevance.

For English titles, aim for 15-25 words unless the user requests otherwise.
